Checked 2026-11-05. These are the **dataset/license statements in the upstream model cards**, not a blanket license for every output or a conclusion about the legal effect of fine-tuning. Several cards inherit training from other voices, link to separate terms or do not give a standalone weight license. Review the linked card and original dataset before redistributing a voice or using generated audio commercially. No Piper weights are bundled in the plugin or this repository.

Known noncommercial dataset terms include **Ryan, Semaine, HFC female/male and L2-ARCTIC**. Their presence as optional user-selected downloads does not make the whole plugin noncommercial, and does not grant permission to redistribute them or use their audio in promotions. Keep demos on the separately cleared source or Kokoro. CC BY/CC BY-SA voices have attribution/share-alike conditions as described by their actual license; `See URL` and Lessac references need the linked terms, not an assumed MIT/Apache license.

The 28 Kokoro English voices are separately described by [Kokoro's Apache-2.0 model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M). The full catalog has 66 English voices: 28 Kokoro and 38 Piper entries. Review upstream voice counts if the catalog changes.
